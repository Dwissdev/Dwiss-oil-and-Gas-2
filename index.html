Exploration: Where Energy Discovery Begins.

Every oil and gas opportunity begins with a question: what lies beneath the surface? Exploration teams combine geology, geophysics, seismic data, technology and experience to investigate areas that may contain commercially viable energy resources. Exploration can require years of technical analysis, evaluation and investment before a discovery can progress into development.

How Do We Find Oil and Gas?

01 — Understand the Geology

Scientists study the geological history and structure of an area.

02 — Collect Seismic Data

Advanced seismic techniques help create images of geological structures beneath the Earth's surface.

03 — Identify the Four Key Elements

Source Rock, Reservoir Rock, Seal and Trap are evaluated to determine hydrocarbon potential.

04 — Analyze the Evidence

Geologists and geophysicists combine sources of information to determine if further investigation is warranted.

05 — Evaluate the Opportunity

Technical, commercial, environmental and regulatory factors are considered before development decisions are made.

From the Rocks to the Energy We Use.

The journey from an underground resource to usable energy involves thousands of decisions, specialized professionals and complex operations.
